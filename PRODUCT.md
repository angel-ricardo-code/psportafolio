# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS

## Users

Clients, creators, and brands seeking professional video editing, color grading, and motion graphics services.

## Product Purpose

To showcase the video editing, post-production, and color grading portfolio of Angel Ricardo Carvajal Díaz, demonstrating expertise, before/after transformations, and professional quality.

## Positioning

A minimalist, ultra-refined multimedia catalog focused on high-end video editing craftsmanship with intuitive before/after comparisons and seamless contact access.

## Operating Context

Evaluated on desktop and mobile web browsers by prospective clients looking for a skilled video editor and post-producer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single-file responsive static web page containing an interactive profile showcase, before/after editing examples, video player integration, and direct contact options.

## Brand Commitments

Angel Ricardo Carvajal Díaz brand identity, minimalist Apple-inspired aesthetic, clean typography (Inter, JetBrains Mono), polished interactions, and high-fidelity media presentation.

## Evidence on Hand
- Profile image (`images/profile.avif`)
- Before/after editing sample images (`images/01-before.avif`, `images/01-after.avif`, etc.)

## Product Principles
1. **Craft First:** Precision and immaculate visual hierarchy over clutter.
2. **Immediate Clarity:** Instantly showcase professional work and proof through before/after media.
3. **Seamless Contact:** Effortless conversion paths for prospective clients.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic HTML structure and responsive design for readability across modern viewports.
